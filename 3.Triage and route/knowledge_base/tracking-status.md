# Tracking a report's status

Every ticket moves through a five-step timeline on the tracking page:
**Submitted → Under Review → Routed → In Progress → Resolved**.

- **Submitted**: your report has been received and is being processed by
  the Language Normalizer and Evidence Extractor stages.
- **Under Review**: a human operator (the "DNO") is reviewing the ticket in
  the Triage and Route queue, alongside its priority score and duplicate
  status.
- **Routed**: the operator has made the Track A (routine ULB repair) or
  Track B (research/innovation) call, and the ticket has been handed to the
  matching downstream stage.
- **In Progress**: the ULB department (Track A) or the matched institution
  team (Track B) is actively working the issue.
- **Resolved**: Track A closes once the ULB reports the fix and closure
  proof is verified; Track B closes once its pilot validation passes.

A ticket that turns out to be a duplicate of an existing one is instead
marked **merged** — it does not follow the five-step timeline on its own,
because it has been folded into the original ticket's progress. Merging
several reports of the same underlying issue into one ticket also raises
that ticket's priority, since it reflects more people affected.

You can look up any ticket's current status by its ticket ID on the
tracking page.
